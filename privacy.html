Privacy Policy

CadePact is built to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the CadePact app on iPhone, what stays on your devices, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who we are

CadePact ("CadePact", "we", "us") makes the CadePact app, which offers synthesized sounds, breathing exercises, a sleep journal, a to-do list for tomorrow and a wake-up alarm for sleep, focus and relaxation.

If you have any question about this policy or your information, you can reach us at cadepact@gmail.com.

2. The short version

CadePact has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that collect your data, and we never sell, rent or share your personal information. What you create in the app stays on your devices and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud account.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.

The app does not send your usage, your sounds or mixes, your journal entries, your notes, your to-do items or your sleep data to us or to anyone else. All sounds are generated live on your device; playing them needs no internet connection.

4. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps some information on your device: your preferences (such as bedtime, wake-up time, timer length, session mode, the sound or mix you last played and its volumes), your sleep journal entries and notes, your to-do list for tomorrow, and whether reminders and the alarm are turned on.

This information is stored using Apple's standard on-device storage and is protected by your device's passcode and iOS data protection. We cannot see it.

5. iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for CadePact in your device settings, your journal and to-do list are synced between your own devices through your private iCloud account (CloudKit). This sync is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The data is stored in your personal iCloud space, not on any server of ours, and we have no access to it.

You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

6. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, CadePact asks for permission to read your sleep analysis (for example, sleep recorded by Apple Watch or another sleep tracker) so it can fill in your sleep journal. CadePact only reads this data; it never writes anything to Apple Health.

Health data is used only inside the app, on your device, to show your sleep. It is never sent to us, never shared with or sold to third parties, never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never stored in iCloud by CadePact. This follows Apple's HealthKit rules.

You can stop CadePact reading Health data at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → CadePact, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

7. Notifications and alarms

If you turn them on, CadePact schedules a wind-down reminder and a wake-up alarm on your device. They are created and delivered by iOS (notifications and AlarmKit); no information leaves your device to schedule them. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Widgets, Live Activities and the Dynamic Island can show details such as the sound or mix playing, the timer, your schedule and your to-do items. This information is prepared on your device and is not sent to us, but it may be visible to anyone who can see your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can remove widgets at any time, and turn off Live Activities in iOS Settings → CadePact.

8. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card, billing address or Apple Account details. The app only learns from Apple whether you have an active trial, subscription or purchase, so it can unlock CadePact Pro. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking

CadePact contains no third-party software development kits for analytics, advertising, attribution or crash reporting. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not use your information for targeted advertising. Because we do not track you, there is nothing for a "Do Not Track" signal to turn off.

If you choose to share crash reports and analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash information. It does not identify you.

10. How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your information stays on your devices (and in your iCloud, if sync is on) until you delete it. You are always in control:

• In the app, go to Settings → Delete All Data to remove your journal, to-do list and preferences from this device and, through sync, from your iCloud.

• Journal nights and to-do items you delete wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can restore them. After that they are removed for good. You can also remove them sooner from Recently Deleted.

• Deleting the app removes the information stored on that device.

• You can also manage or delete CadePact's iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

11. Security

Because your information stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account, it benefits from Apple's built-in protections, including device encryption, passcode and Face ID or Touch ID, and the encryption iCloud applies to synced data. Please keep your device passcode and Apple Account secure.

12. Children

CadePact is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information, it complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights in the United States

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws in other U.S. states give you rights to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising.

CadePact does not collect personal information on its servers, and we do not sell or share personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The information the app keeps is on your device and in your iCloud, where you can view, change and delete it yourself at any time. If you have a question or a request, write to cadepact@gmail.com; we will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

14. Your rights in Canada

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and provincial laws, including Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), you have the right to access and correct your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to be told how it is handled.

Because CadePact keeps your information only on your devices and in your own iCloud, you can access, correct and delete it directly in the app and in iOS Settings. For any question, or to reach the person responsible for privacy at CadePact, write to cadepact@gmail.com. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

15. Users in other countries

CadePact is offered on the App Store in the United States and Canada. If you use it elsewhere, the same practices apply: your information stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, and you keep any rights your local law gives you.

16. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page. If a change ever affected how your information is handled, we would tell you in the app before it takes effect.

17. Contact

Questions, requests or concerns about privacy? Write to cadepact@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
